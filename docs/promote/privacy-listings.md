## Standaard-bouwstenen (overal te hergebruiken)

**Naam:** FotoApp

**Tagline (1 regel):**
> Free, open-source, local-only photo & video manager — no cloud, no account, no subscription.

**Korte beschrijving (±240 tekens):**
> FotoApp organizes your entire photo and video collection on your own computer. Duplicate detection, GPS map, RAW support and Google Takeout import — fully offline. A privacy-first alternative to Google Photos and Apple Photos.

**Lange beschrijving:**
> FotoApp is a free, open-source desktop app for managing photos and videos entirely on your own machine — no cloud, no account, no subscription. It scans your drives, detects duplicates (MD5 hash), shows geotagged photos on an OpenStreetMap map, supports RAW files (Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fuji), and imports Google Takeout exports to recover original dates and GPS. Smart export renames files like `Netherlands_Amsterdam_15_06_2023.jpg` and sorts them by year/month. Built with Node.js, SQLite and Electron. Runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

**Categorie:** Photo Management / Photo Organizer / Gallery

**License:** GPL-3.0 (open source)

**Platforms:** Windows, macOS, Linux (self-hosted/local)

**Links:**
- Website: https://boulbaal.github.io/fotoApp/
- Source: https://github.com/boulbaal/fotoApp
- Downloads: https://github.com/boulbaal/fotoApp/releases/latest

**Alternatief voor:** Google Photos, Apple Photos, digiKam, Shotwell, Adobe Lightroom

## 1. Privacy Guides — forum (discuss.privacyguides.net)

**Manier:** Forum-post (eigen account, login vereist — jij plaatst zelf).
**Status:** ⚠️ Waarschijnlijk te vroeg. Privacy Guides hanteert strenge volwassenheidseisen (actieve community, audits, langere bestaansgeschiedenis). Een repo van 1 week met 0 sterren wordt vrijwel zeker afgewezen of genegeerd. **Advies: wachten tot je echte gebruikers/sterren hebt (paar maanden), dán plaatsen.**

> Full transparency: it's a young project (released June 2026), so I understand if it's premature for the main list. Sharing it for feedback and visibility. Source: https://github.com/boulbaal/fotoApp
