Google Photos toevoegen

Google Photos cannot be connected directly. First export your photos via Google Takeout:

  1. Go to takeout.google.com
  2. Select only "Google Photos"
  3. Download the ZIP files (can take hours)
  4. Extract everything into one folder on your PC
  5. Add that folder below as a source

✅ De app leest automatisch de Google-metadata mee: date, GPS-location, beschrijving en apparaat.
